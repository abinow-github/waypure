What does Waypure offer?+

Our range includes water purifiers, air purifiers, ozone detoxifiers and filtration services, with domestic and commercial solutions. You can also enquire about the treatment, storage and energy systems listed above.

Which brands can I explore?+

The catalogue includes Waypure, Aquaguard, Merlin, Prolife and Chrome models. Use the brand filters to compare the listed products and contact us for current availability.

Are these the current selling prices?+

The displayed MRPs come from the October 2023 catalogue. Contact Waypure to confirm the current price and availability of your chosen model.

What warranty is listed for these models?+

The catalogue lists 36 months for many Waypure models and a lifetime stainless steel tank warranty for selected Aquaguard models. Coverage varies by model and component; confirm the applicable terms with Waypure when you enquire.
